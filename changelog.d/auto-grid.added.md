Auto Multi-View (`MULTIVIEW_AUTO_GRID=1`): a "Multi-View" channel driven from
the Jellyfin app. Open two or more channels and then Multi-View for a grid of
those; open one to add it to the current grid; open none to get the last grid
back. Also Intel Quick Sync decoding for Multi-View (`MULTIVIEW_HWACCEL=qsv`);
the Docker image includes jellyfin-ffmpeg for it.
